# Easel Studio 0.0.8

Strudel gets an offline drum bank, support for attached audio samples and fixes
for WAV export. Export WAV and Cycles now sit in the top toolbar.

- Play and export seven bundled drum samples: kick (`bd`), snare (`sd`), closed
  and open hats (`hh`/`oh`), clap (`cp`), tom and rim. The original samples are
  generated reproducibly and work without a network connection.
- Attach short WAV or MP3 audio from Media, including Suno output, and register
  it with `EaselStrudelSamples.add('sample_name', 'MEDIA_ASSET_ID')`. Keep that
  registration in the score so it works after reopening. Adding samples and
  evaluating an updated score preserve the live audio context.
- Export native kick/noise sounds and registered samples with the correct
  envelopes, including full attack-only sample tails. Exports also work when
  the playback device runs at 44.1 kHz.
- Keep live media attachment working after canvas fragment navigation, without
  forcing a source reload.
- Explain unavailable sample features when adding a new template to a project
  with an older pinned Strudel kit; synth examples remain usable.

Create a new Strudel project from Templates to get the bundled drums. Existing
projects keep their authored source, media and pinned kits.

Attached samples support mono/stereo WAV or MP3, up to 10 seconds and 4 MiB each.
WAV export supports 1-16 cycles and up to 30 seconds, with at most 16 sample
names and 8 MiB of sample input. Remote banks, effects, and sample pitch, speed
or slicing remain outside the supported WAV export subset.

Version 0.0.8 also retains the Windows template and timeline storage fix from
0.0.7.
